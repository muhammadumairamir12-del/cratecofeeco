FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I try the gym before joining?
Absolutely! We offer a free 1-day trial for all new visitors. No payment needed. Book it through our booking page or WhatsApp us directly.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, bank transfer, and most major debit/credit cards. Our front desk team will assist you with all payment options.
Can I upgrade my membership later?
Yes, you can upgrade at any time. Downgrades are permitted at the end of your current billing cycle. Contact our membership team for assistance.
Are personal training sessions included in all plans?
PT sessions are included in 3-Month, 6-Month, and Yearly packages. Extra sessions can be purchased separately for Monthly members.
